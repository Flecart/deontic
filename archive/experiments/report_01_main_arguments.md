## The motion
*Does a legal/deontic-logic approach — an explicit, auditable, defeasible normative layer —
have a real "take" as institutional infrastructure for self-interested AI agent societies,
or is effort better spent on non-symbolic alignment (mech-interp, steering vectors, debate)?*

## The two camps
- **Angelo (proponent):** alignment-*by-institution*. Inter-agent norms (breach, delegation,
  conflicting obligations) are relational facts not located in any one model's weights, so they
  need an external, inspectable, machine-checkable layer *over* capable reasoners.
- **Skeptic:** alignment-*by-internals* and capability-scaling oversight. The calculus is old;
  the hard part is feeding it; symbolic layers are brittle and don't ride the bitter lesson.

## What is settled (with evidence)
1. **The engine's value is specific, not "smarter law."** Verdict accuracy is a statistical tie
   (100% vs 96%, overlapping CIs). The real win is **norm-identification**: offence-ID 83% vs 33%
   (non-overlapping Wilson CIs even at n=18); RAG 0%.
2. **Circularity broken cross-family.** Independent annotators from two model families corroborate
   the engine's gold at **83–89%** (gpt-4.1 89%, DeepSeek 83%).
3. **Enforced lex specialis is model-independent.** The premeditation→`Ergastolo` escalation is
   missed by *both* LLM families; only the engine catches it.
4. **Commitment device works.** On a contract breach, two self-interested agents reach opposite
   verdicts; the engine yields one both can pre-commit to.
5. **Auditability holds.** Across every formalization failure, theories fail *loud* (loader rejects)
   or *legible* (readable in the `.ddl`) — never silently wrong.

## What was conceded or refuted (intellectual honesty)
- **The skeptic won the formalization round (C2):** unscaffolded text→DDL is unreliable (0/3);
  E4's success was scaffolding-driven.
- **Angelo retracted** the strong "LLMs can't identify offences" claim (~80% of the LLM's offence-ID
  "misses" were correct reasoning under non-canonical names) **and** the minimal-pair "consistency
  win" (CIs overlap at n=11).
- **The "legalism underperforms" attack was refuted** (it conflated fixed-constitution with the
  common-law arm).

## The one open frontier (at this point)
**C2 — formalization fidelity at scale.** It *decomposes* into a cheap **syntax layer**
(scaffolding-solved: 0/3→2/2) and an expensive **semantics layer** that generalizes from operator
*definitions* even out-of-distribution (CTD: pass) but breaks on **compound-effect clauses** at
multi-clause scale (E10: 4/5).
