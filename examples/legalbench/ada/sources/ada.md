# ada — Americans with Disabilities Act (deontic duties of covered entities)
no-discrimination: A covered entity shall not discriminate against a qualified individual on the basis of disability.
reasonable-accommodation: Upon request, an employer shall provide a reasonable accommodation to a qualified individual with a disability.
accessibility: A place of public accommodation shall remove architectural barriers where readily achievable.
interactive-process: Upon an accommodation request, an employer shall engage in an interactive process to identify accommodations.
