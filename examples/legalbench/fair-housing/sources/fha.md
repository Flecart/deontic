# fair-housing — Fair Housing Act (deontic duties of housing providers/agents)
no-refusal: A housing provider shall not refuse to rent or sell a dwelling on a protected basis.
discriminatory-terms: A housing provider shall not impose different terms or conditions on a protected basis.
discriminatory-advertising: A housing provider shall not make any discriminatory statement or advertisement.
reasonable-accommodation: Upon request, a housing provider shall provide a reasonable accommodation for a person with a disability.
no-steering: A real estate agent shall not steer prospective buyers on a protected basis.
