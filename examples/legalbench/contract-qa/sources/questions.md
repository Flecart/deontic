# contract_qa — representative clauses behind yes/no questions (deontic subset)

overtime: The employer shall pay overtime for hours worked beyond 40 per week.
confidentiality: The employee shall not disclose the employer's trade secrets.
reimbursement: The employer shall reimburse approved business expenses.
