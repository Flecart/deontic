# privacy_policy — representative clauses (LegalBench privacy_policy_* deontic subset)
breach-notification: The company shall notify affected users without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach.
deletion: Upon the user's request, the company shall delete the user's personal data.
third-party-sharing: The company may share personal data with third-party service providers to operate the service.
access: Upon the user's request, the company shall provide the user access to their personal data.
