# ecoa — Equal Credit Opportunity Act (deontic duties of creditors)
no-discrimination: A creditor shall not discriminate against an applicant on a prohibited basis in any aspect of a credit transaction.
adverse-action-notice: A creditor shall provide written notice of adverse action when it denies or revokes credit.
no-discouragement: A creditor shall not discourage, on a prohibited basis, a prospective applicant from applying for credit.
protected-income: A creditor shall not disregard income derived from public assistance in evaluating creditworthiness.
