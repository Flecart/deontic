# maud — merger-agreement deal points (LegalBench maud_*; deontic subset)
ordinary-course: Between signing and closing, the target shall conduct its business in the ordinary course.
no-shop: The target shall not solicit alternative acquisition proposals.
efforts: Each party shall use reasonable best efforts to obtain the required regulatory approvals.
fiduciary-out: The board may change its recommendation in response to a superior proposal.
financing-condition: The buyer shall close even if it fails to obtain financing (no financing condition).
mae-termination: A party may terminate the agreement if the other suffers a material adverse effect.
hell-or-high-water: The buyer shall take any action required by antitrust authorities to obtain clearance.
