# coppa — Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (deontic duties of operators)
parental-consent: An operator shall obtain verifiable parental consent before collecting personal information from a child under 13.
notice: An operator shall provide notice of its information practices regarding children.
parental-access: Upon a parent's request, an operator shall provide the parent access to the child's personal information.
parental-deletion: Upon a parent's request, an operator shall delete the child's personal information.
no-conditioning: An operator shall not condition a child's participation in an activity on disclosing more information than is reasonably necessary.
