# can-spam — CAN-SPAM Act (deontic duties of commercial-email senders)
false-header: A sender shall not use false or misleading header information in a commercial email.
deceptive-subject: A sender shall not use a deceptive or misleading subject line.
opt-out: A sender shall provide a clear and functioning opt-out mechanism.
honor-opt-out: A sender shall not send commercial email to a recipient after they have opted out.
physical-address: A sender shall include a valid physical postal address in the email.
