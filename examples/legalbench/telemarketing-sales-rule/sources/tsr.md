# telemarketing_sales_rule — representative duties (FTC TSR; LegalBench tsr task)
do-not-call: A telemarketer shall not call any number on the National Do Not Call Registry.
identification: A telemarketer shall promptly disclose the seller's identity and that the call is a sales call.
calling-hours: A telemarketer shall not initiate an outbound call before 8 a.m. or after 9 p.m. local time.
robocall-consent: A telemarketer shall not deliver a prerecorded message without the recipient's prior express written consent.
abandoned-calls: A telemarketer shall not abandon more than three percent of answered calls.
caller-id: A telemarketer shall transmit accurate caller identification information.
