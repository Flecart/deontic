# supply_chain_disclosure — CA Transparency in Supply Chains Act (SB 657)
# (LegalBench supply_chain_disclosure task; disclosure duties of covered retailers)
verification: A covered retailer shall disclose the extent to which it verifies product supply chains to evaluate risks of human trafficking and slavery.
audits: A covered retailer shall disclose the extent to which it audits suppliers for compliance.
certification: A covered retailer shall disclose whether it requires suppliers to certify that materials comply with anti-trafficking laws.
internal-accountability: A covered retailer shall disclose whether it maintains internal accountability standards for employees failing to meet anti-trafficking standards.
training: A covered retailer shall disclose whether it trains employees on mitigating trafficking risks in supply chains.
