# securities — federal securities-law duties (deontic subset)
insider-trading: An insider shall not trade securities on the basis of material nonpublic information.
tipping: An insider shall not disclose material nonpublic information to others who may trade on it.
reg-fd: Upon a selective disclosure of material nonpublic information, the issuer shall make prompt public disclosure.
section16-reporting: An insider shall report a change in beneficial ownership on Form 4 within two business days.
