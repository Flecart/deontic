# consumer_contracts_qa — representative clauses behind yes/no questions
# (LegalBench consumer_contracts_qa; deontic subset)
cancel: The consumer may cancel the subscription at any time without penalty.
refund: The company shall refund the consumer if the service is unavailable for more than 24 hours.
unilateral-change: The company may modify these terms at any time at its sole discretion.
arbitration: The consumer must submit any dispute to binding arbitration.
late-fee: The company may charge a late fee if payment is not received by the due date.
auto-renewal: The subscription shall automatically renew for successive terms unless the consumer cancels before renewal.
data-opt-out: The consumer may opt out of the sale of their personal information at any time.
