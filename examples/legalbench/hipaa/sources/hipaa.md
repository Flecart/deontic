# hipaa — HIPAA Privacy Rule (deontic duties of covered entities re PHI)
minimum-necessary: A covered entity shall limit PHI use and disclosure to the minimum necessary.
authorization: A covered entity may disclose PHI for a non-permitted purpose only with the patient's authorization.
breach-notification: A covered entity shall notify affected individuals of a breach of unsecured PHI.
accounting: Upon the patient's request, a covered entity shall provide an accounting of disclosures.
