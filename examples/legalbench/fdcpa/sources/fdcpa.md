# fdcpa — Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (deontic duties of debt collectors)
unusual-time: A debt collector shall not communicate with a consumer at an unusual or inconvenient time (before 8am or after 9pm).
harassment: A debt collector shall not harass, oppress, or abuse any person in collecting a debt.
false-representation: A debt collector shall not use any false, deceptive, or misleading representation.
validation-notice: A debt collector shall send the consumer a written validation notice within five days of the initial communication.
cease-communication: If the consumer notifies the collector in writing to cease, the collector shall not further communicate with the consumer.
no-third-party: A debt collector shall not disclose the debt to third parties other than the consumer or their attorney.
