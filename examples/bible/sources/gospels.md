# Source passages (New Testament, public-domain KJV/ASV phrasing)

Line-addressable so `.ddl` atoms and rules can cite a span, e.g.
`sources/gospels.md#L10-L12`. Quotations are abbreviated to the operative clause.

## The two great commandments — the supreme principle

Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy heart, and with all thy soul, and
with all thy mind. This is the first and great commandment.
And the second is like unto it, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.
On these two commandments hang all the law and the prophets. (Matt 22:37-40)

The golden rule: All things whatsoever ye would that men should do to you, do ye
even so to them: for this is the law and the prophets. (Matt 7:12)

## The law is not abolished but fulfilled / re-ordered

Think not that I am come to destroy the law, or the prophets: I am not come to
destroy, but to fulfil. (Matt 5:17)

The sabbath was made for man, and not man for the sabbath. (Mark 2:27)

I will have mercy, and not sacrifice. (Matt 9:13; 12:7)

## The Decalogue / written commandments (object-level duties)

Thou shalt not kill. Thou shalt not commit adultery. Thou shalt not steal.
Thou shalt not bear false witness. Honour thy father and thy mother. (Matt 19:18-19)

## Episodes — how judgments are made

### Sabbath: plucking grain and the withered hand (Mark 2:23-3:6)
Is it lawful to do good on the sabbath days, or to do evil? to save life, or to
kill? ... and he saith unto the man, Stretch forth thine hand. And he stretched
it out: and his hand was restored. (Mark 3:4-5)

### The Good Samaritan (Luke 10:25-37)
A certain man went down from Jerusalem to Jericho, and fell among thieves ... a
certain priest came ... and passed by on the other side. ... But a certain
Samaritan ... had compassion on him ... Go, and do thou likewise. (Luke 10:30-37)

### The woman taken in adultery (John 8:3-11)
He that is without sin among you, let him first cast a stone at her. ... Neither
do I condemn thee: go, and sin no more. (John 8:7-11)

### Zacchaeus — restitution (Luke 19:8-9)
Behold, Lord, the half of my goods I give to the poor; and if I have taken any
thing from any man by false accusation, I restore him fourfold. ... This day is
salvation come to this house. (Luke 19:8-9)

### Render unto Caesar — the two jurisdictions (Mark 12:14-17)
Is it lawful to give tribute to Caesar, or not? ... Render to Caesar the things
that are Caesar's, and to God the things that are God's. (Mark 12:14-17)

### The rich young ruler (Mark 10:17-22)
Thou knowest the commandments ... go thy way, sell whatsoever thou hast, and give
to the poor ... and come, take up the cross, and follow me. (Mark 10:19-21)

### The sheep and the goats — the criterion of judgment (Matt 25:31-46)
Inasmuch as ye have done it unto one of the least of these my brethren, ye have
done it unto me. ... Inasmuch as ye did it not to one of the least of these, ye
did it not to me. (Matt 25:40-45)

### Cleansing the temple (Mark 11:15-17)
And Jesus went into the temple, and began to cast out them that sold and bought
in the temple ... My house shall be called of all nations the house of prayer;
but ye have made it a den of thieves. (Mark 11:15-17)

### Healing, forgiving, and the sinful woman (Luke 7:37-50)
Her sins, which are many, are forgiven; for she loved much. ... Thy faith hath
saved thee; go in peace. (Luke 7:47-50)

### Corban — duty to parents over ritual vow (Mark 7:9-13)
Ye ... reject the commandment of God, that ye may keep your own tradition. ...
making the word of God of none effect through your tradition. (Mark 7:9-13)

### Plucking out the eye / turning the other cheek (Matt 5:29,39)
If thy right eye offend thee, pluck it out ... whosoever shall smite thee on thy
right cheek, turn to him the other also. (Matt 5:29,39)
