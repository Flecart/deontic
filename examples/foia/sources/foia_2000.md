# Freedom of Information Act 2000 — source excerpts

Verbatim excerpts (as in force, post-DPA 2018 amendments) from
https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/36. Only the provisions formalized in
`../foia.ddl` are included; `[...]` marks omitted text. Atom provenance selectors
point into this file — keep line positions stable (append, don't reflow).

## Section 1 — General right of access to information held by public authorities

s1(1): "Any person making a request for information to a public authority is
entitled—(a) to be informed in writing by the public authority whether it holds
information of the description specified in the request, and (b) if that is the
case, to have that information communicated to him."

s1(2): "Subsection (1) has effect subject to the following provisions of this
section and to the provisions of sections 2, 9, 12 and 14."

s1(6): the duty of a public authority to comply with subsection (1)(a) is
referred to as "the duty to confirm or deny".

## Section 2 — Effect of the exemptions in Part II

s2(1): where a Part II provision states that the duty to confirm or deny does
not arise, the duty does not apply if "(a) the provision confers absolute
exemption, or (b) in all the circumstances of the case, the public interest in
maintaining the exclusion of the duty to confirm or deny outweighs the public
interest in disclosing whether the public authority holds the information."

s2(2): in respect of exempt information, section 1(1)(b) does not apply if
"(a) the information is exempt information by virtue of a provision conferring
absolute exemption, or (b) in all the circumstances of the case, the public
interest in maintaining the exemption outweighs the public interest in
disclosing the information."

s2(3): the following provisions confer absolute exemption — section 21, section
23, section 32, section 34, section 36 (House of Commons / House of Lords
information), section 37(1)(a)–(ab) and related (2), "section 40(1) and 40(2)
where the first condition is satisfied", section 41, section 44.

## Section 17 — Refusal of request

s17(1): a public authority relying on a claim that information is exempt must,
within the time for compliance with section 1(1), give the applicant a notice
which "states that fact, specifies the exemption in question, and states (if
that would not otherwise be apparent) why the exemption applies."

## Section 21 — Information accessible to applicant by other means (ABSOLUTE)

s21(1): "Information which is reasonably accessible to the applicant otherwise
than under section 1 is exempt information."

s21(2): "information may be reasonably accessible to the applicant even though
it is accessible only on payment" [...]

## Section 31 — Law enforcement (QUALIFIED)

s31(1): information is exempt if its disclosure "would, or would be likely to,
prejudice" — "(a) the prevention or detection of crime, (b) the apprehension or
prosecution of offenders, (c) the administration of justice, (d) the assessment
or collection of any tax or duty [...], (e) the operation of the immigration
controls, (f) the maintenance of security and good order in prisons [...]" [...]

s31(3): "The duty to confirm or deny does not arise if, or to the extent that,
compliance with section 1(1)(a) would, or would be likely to, prejudice any of
the matters mentioned in subsection (1)."

## Section 40 — Personal information (ABSOLUTE where first condition satisfied)

s40(1): "Any information to which a request for information relates is exempt
information if it constitutes personal data of which the applicant is the data
subject."

s40(2): information is also exempt if it constitutes personal data of another
person and the first, second or third condition is satisfied.

s40(3A) (first condition): disclosure of the information to a member of the
public otherwise than under this Act "would contravene any of the data
protection principles" (or Article 21 UK GDPR objection cases).

s40(5A): the duty to confirm or deny "does not arise in relation to information
which is (or if it were held by the public authority would be) exempt
information by virtue of subsection (1)".

## Section 42 — Legal professional privilege (QUALIFIED)

s42(1): "Information in respect of which a claim to legal professional
privilege or, in Scotland, to confidentiality of communications could be
maintained in legal proceedings is exempt information."

## Section 43 — Commercial interests (QUALIFIED)

s43(1): "Information is exempt information if it constitutes a trade secret."

s43(2): "Information is exempt information if its disclosure under this Act
would, or would be likely to, prejudice the commercial interests of any person
(including the public authority holding it)."

s43(3): "The duty to confirm or deny does not arise if, or to the extent that,
compliance with section 1(1)(a) would, or would be likely to, prejudice the
interests mentioned in subsection (2)."

## Section 9 — Fees (Part I)

s9(1): an authority "may, within the period for complying with section 1(1),
give the applicant a notice in writing (... a 'fees notice') stating that a fee
of an amount specified in the notice is to be charged".

s9(2): "Where a fees notice has been given to the applicant, the public
authority is not obliged to comply with section 1(1) unless the fee is paid
within the period of three months".

## Section 10 — Time for compliance (Part I)

s10(1): "a public authority must comply with section 1(1) promptly and in any
event not later than the twentieth working day following the date of receipt."

## Section 12 — Cost of compliance exceeds appropriate limit (Part I)

s12(1): "Section 1(1) does not oblige a public authority to comply with a
request for information if the authority estimates that the cost of complying
with the request would exceed the appropriate limit."

## Section 14 — Vexatious or repeated requests (Part I)

s14(1): "Section 1(1) does not oblige a public authority to comply with a
request for information if the request is vexatious."

s14(2): where the authority "has previously complied with a request", it is
not obliged to comply with "a subsequent identical or substantially similar
request from that person unless a reasonable interval has elapsed".

## Section 16 — Advice and assistance (Part I)

s16(1): "It shall be the duty of a public authority to provide advice and
assistance, so far as it would be reasonable to expect the authority to do so,
to persons who propose to make, or have made, requests for information to it."

## Section 22 — Information intended for future publication (QUALIFIED)

s22(1): exempt if "the information is held by the public authority with a view
to its publication, by the authority or any other person, at some future date
(whether determined or not)", the information was already held with that view
when the request was made, and it is reasonable in all the circumstances to
withhold until publication.

## Section 22A — Research (QUALIFIED)

s22A(1): "Information obtained in the course of, or derived from, a programme
of research is exempt information if (a) the programme is continuing with a
view to the publication" of a report, and disclosure before publication would
be likely to prejudice the programme, its participants, or the authority.

## Section 23 — Security bodies (ABSOLUTE)

s23(1): "Information held by a public authority is exempt information if it
was directly or indirectly supplied to the public authority by, or relates to,
any of the bodies specified in subsection (3)" (Security Service, SIS, GCHQ,
special forces, security tribunals, NCA, and related bodies). s23(5) excludes
the duty to confirm or deny where compliance would involve such a disclosure.

## Section 24 — National security (QUALIFIED)

s24(1): information not falling within s23(1) "is exempt information if
exemption from section 1(1)(b) is required for the purpose of safeguarding
national security". s24(2) excludes the duty to confirm or deny if required
for that purpose.

## Section 26 — Defence (QUALIFIED)

s26(1): exempt if disclosure "would, or would be likely to, prejudice (a) the
defence of the British Islands or of any colony, or (b) the capability,
effectiveness or security of any relevant forces".

## Section 27 — International relations (QUALIFIED)

s27(1): exempt if disclosure "would, or would be likely to, prejudice (a)
relations between the United Kingdom and any other State", an international
organisation or court, UK interests abroad, or their promotion or protection;
s27(2) also exempts confidential information obtained from another state or
international organisation/court.

## Section 28 — Relations within the United Kingdom (QUALIFIED)

s28(1): exempt if disclosure "would, or would be likely to, prejudice
relations between any administration in the United Kingdom and any other such
administration" (UK government, Scottish Ministers, Welsh and NI executives).

## Section 29 — The economy (QUALIFIED)

s29(1): exempt if disclosure "would, or would be likely to, prejudice (a) the
economic interests of the United Kingdom or of any part of the United
Kingdom", or the financial interests of any UK administration.

## Section 30 — Investigations and proceedings (QUALIFIED)

s30(1): exempt if held at any time for "(a) any investigation which the public
authority has a duty to conduct with a view to it being ascertained (i)
whether a person should be charged with an offence", or criminal proceedings
the authority conducts; s30(2) also covers information relating to confidential
sources. s30(3) excludes the duty to confirm or deny.

## Section 32 — Court records (ABSOLUTE)

s32(1): exempt if held "only by virtue of being contained in (a) any document
filed with, or otherwise placed in the custody of, a court for the purposes of
proceedings", served documents, or documents created by a court or its staff
(likewise inquiries and arbitrations, s32(2)).

## Section 33 — Audit functions (QUALIFIED)

s33(1)-(2): for authorities with functions of audit of other public
authorities' accounts or examination of their economy/efficiency/
effectiveness, information is exempt if disclosure "would, or would be likely
to, prejudice the exercise of any of the authority's functions" in those
relations.

## Section 34 — Parliamentary privilege (ABSOLUTE)

s34(1): exempt if exemption "is required for the purpose of avoiding an
infringement of the privileges of either House of Parliament".

## Section 35 — Formulation of government policy (QUALIFIED)

s35(1): information held by a government department (or Welsh Government) is
exempt if it relates to "(a) the formulation or development of government
policy, (b) Ministerial communications, (c) the provision of advice by any of
the Law Officers..., or (d) the operation of any Ministerial private office".

## Section 36 — Prejudice to effective conduct of public affairs (QUALIFIED)

s36(2): exempt if, "in the reasonable opinion of a qualified person",
disclosure "(a) would, or would be likely to, prejudice (i) the maintenance of
the convention of the collective responsibility of Ministers", "(b) would, or
would be likely to, inhibit (i) the free and frank provision of advice, or
(ii) the free and frank exchange of views for the purposes of deliberation, or
(c) would otherwise prejudice... the effective conduct of public affairs".
(Absolute only for information held by the Commons or Lords.)

## Section 37 — Royal communications and honours (PART ABSOLUTE)

s37(1): exempt if it relates to "(a) communications with the Sovereign, (aa)
communications with the heir to, or the person who is for the time being
second in line of succession to, the Throne" (absolute), "(ac) communications
with other members of the Royal Family or Household" (qualified), or "(b) the
conferring by the Crown of any honour or dignity" (qualified).

## Section 38 — Health and safety (QUALIFIED)

s38(1): exempt if disclosure "would, or would be likely to (a) endanger the
physical or mental health of any individual, or (b) endanger the safety of any
individual".

## Section 39 — Environmental information (QUALIFIED)

s39(1): exempt if the authority "(a) is obliged by environmental information
regulations to make the information available to the public in accordance with
the regulations, or (b) would be so obliged but for any exemption contained in
the regulations" (the request is handled under the EIR regime instead).

## Section 41 — Information provided in confidence (ABSOLUTE)

s41(1): exempt if "(a) it was obtained by the public authority from any other
person (including another public authority), and (b) the disclosure of the
information to the public (otherwise than under this Act) by the public
authority holding it would constitute a breach of confidence actionable by
that or any other person".

## Section 44 — Prohibitions on disclosure (ABSOLUTE)

s44(1): exempt if its disclosure (otherwise than under this Act) "(a) is
prohibited by or under any enactment, (b) is incompatible with any assimilated
obligation, or (c) would constitute or be punishable as a contempt of court".

## Section 7 — public authorities to which Act has limited application; Schedule 1 Part VI designation limits

> 7(1) Where a public authority is listed in Schedule 1 only in relation to
> information of a specified description, nothing in Parts I to V of this Act
> applies to any other information held by the authority.

> Schedule 1, Part VI: The British Broadcasting Corporation, in respect of
> information held for purposes other than those of journalism, art or
> literature.
