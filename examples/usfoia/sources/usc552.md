# 5 U.S.C. § 552 (US FOIA) — source excerpts

Excerpts from https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/5/552. Keep line
positions stable.

## (a)(3)(A) — the duty

"Each agency [...] shall make the records promptly available to any person"
upon a request that reasonably describes the records and complies with
published procedures.

## (a)(8)(A) — the foreseeable-harm standard

An agency "shall withhold information [...] only if — (I) the agency
reasonably foresees that disclosure would harm an interest protected by an
exemption described in subsection (b); or (II) disclosure is prohibited by
law."

## (b) — the nine exemptions

(1) matters properly classified under an Executive order in the interest of
national defense or foreign policy; (2) records related solely to internal
personnel rules and practices of an agency; (3) matters specifically exempted
from disclosure by another statute; (4) trade secrets and commercial or
financial information obtained from a person and privileged or confidential;
(5) inter-agency or intra-agency memorandums or letters that would not be
available by law to a party in litigation with the agency (deliberative
process, attorney-client, attorney work-product privileges); (6) personnel
and medical files and similar files the disclosure of which would constitute
a clearly unwarranted invasion of personal privacy; (7) records or
information compiled for law enforcement purposes, to the extent disclosure
(A) could interfere with enforcement proceedings, (C) could constitute an
unwarranted invasion of personal privacy, (D) could disclose a confidential
source, (E) would disclose techniques and procedures, or (F) could endanger
life or physical safety; (8) matters in examination reports of agencies
regulating financial institutions; (9) geological and geophysical information
and data concerning wells.

## (b) closing sentence — segregability

"Any reasonably segregable portion of a record shall be provided to any
person requesting such record after deletion of the portions which are
exempt under this subsection."
