# Enterprise SaaS Master Services Agreement (synthetic composite source)

This file backs atom `uri:` selectors for `enterprise_saas_msa.ddl`. It compresses
typical MSA + Order Form + DPA themes into excerpt-length clauses.

## 1. License and use

Upon execution of an Order Form, Vendor grants Customer a non-exclusive,
non-transferable license to use the Software during the Subscription Term solely
for Customer's internal business purposes. Customer shall not exceed the scope
of use stated in the Order Form. Any use outside that scope is a material breach
and may result in suspension or termination.

## 2. Fees and suspension

Customer shall pay all fees when due. If payment is more than thirty (30) days
overdue, Vendor may suspend access to the Software until Customer pays all overdue
amounts; upon payment, Vendor shall restore access within two (2) business days.

## 3. Service levels

During an active SLA, Vendor shall maintain monthly uptime of at least 99.9%.
If Vendor fails to meet uptime in a calendar month, Customer may claim service
credits as Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for that SLA failure.

## 4. Confidentiality

Each party shall not disclose the other party's Confidential Information except
(a) to its employees who have a need to know and who are bound by obligations
no less protective than this Agreement, or (b) where disclosure is required by
law or court order (provided the disclosing party gives prompt notice where
legally permitted).

## 5. Data processing

Vendor shall process Personal Data only on documented instructions from Customer.
Vendor shall not engage a subprocessor without thirty (30) days' prior notice to
Customer, during which Customer may object on reasonable grounds.

## 6. Security incidents

Vendor shall implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures.
Upon becoming aware of a Security Incident affecting Customer Personal Data,
Vendor shall notify Customer without undue delay and, where feasible, within
seventy-two (72) hours, and shall take reasonable steps to remediate the incident.

## 7. Indemnification and liability cap

Vendor shall indemnify Customer against third-party claims arising from Vendor's
breach of the Agreement or infringement of a third party's intellectual property
rights by the Software. Except for Excluded Liabilities, each party's aggregate
liability arising out of or related to the Agreement is capped at the fees paid
by Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim. Excluded Liabilities
include fraud, wilful misconduct, breach of confidentiality obligations, and
intellectual property infringement indemnity obligations.

## 8. Termination and wind-down

Either party may terminate for convenience on ninety (90) days' written notice.
Either party may terminate for cause upon material breach not cured within thirty
(30) days after notice. Upon termination, Vendor shall return or delete Customer
Data as instructed and provide a limited wind-down period for export.

## 9. Non-solicitation

During the term and for twelve (12) months thereafter, Customer shall not solicit
for employment any Vendor employee with whom Customer had material contact,
except where the employee initiates contact without solicitation.

## 10. Insurance and audit

Vendor shall maintain commercially reasonable insurance. Upon reasonable notice,
Customer may audit Vendor's compliance with the security and data processing
obligations no more than once per year.
