# Finance Act 2009, Schedule 55 — source excerpts

Verbatim excerpts from https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/10/schedule/55
(penalty for failure to make returns). Keep line positions stable.

## Paragraph 1 — the penalty trigger

"A penalty is payable by a person ("P") where P fails to make or deliver a
return [...] on or before the filing date."

## Paragraph 3 — fixed penalty

"P is liable to a penalty under this paragraph of £100."

## Paragraphs 4–6 — escalating penalties

Para 4: £10 daily for up to 90 days once the failure continues after 3 months
(if HMRC so decide and give notice). Para 5: the greater of 5% of the tax
liability and £300 after 6 months. Para 6: the greater of a tax-geared
percentage and £300 after 12 months.

## Paragraph 16 — special circumstances

"If HMRC think it right because of special circumstances, they may reduce a
penalty under any paragraph of this Schedule." (Tribunal interferes only if
HMRC's special-circumstances decision was flawed.)

## Paragraph 23 — reasonable excuse

Liability does not arise if P satisfies HMRC or the tribunal that there is a
reasonable excuse for the failure. "(a) an insufficiency of funds is not a
reasonable excuse, unless attributable to events outside P's control,
(b) where P relies on any other person to do anything, that is not a
reasonable excuse unless P took reasonable care to avoid the failure,
(c) where P had a reasonable excuse for the failure but the excuse has
ceased, P is to be treated as having continued to have the excuse if the
failure is remedied without unreasonable delay after the excuse ceased."
